Emotionally Focused Therapy

Understanding the dance between you

EFT treats relationship distress as a protest against disconnection, not a failure. Together we slow down the moments that go wrong, so that new, safer ways of reaching for each other can take hold.

  1. Stage one

    Recognise the cycle

    We map the pattern that pulls you apart and learn to see it, rather than each other, as the problem.

  2. Stage two

    Reach what lies beneath

    We gently explore the fears and needs under the reactions: the parts that rarely get said out loud.

  3. Stage three

    Build new moments of connection

    You practise turning toward each other in ways that feel safe and that last beyond the therapy room.

About Helena

Warm, unhurried and deeply curious about how we love

I'm Helena, a clinical psychotherapist with eighteen years of experience helping people understand how they love, how they protect themselves and how they long for closeness.

Before opening Kindred Room, I worked in community mental health and at a relationship counselling service in Sydney's inner west. I'll meet you where you are, with curiosity rather than judgement, and help you build the kind of connection that feels like home.
