Emotionally Focused Therapy

Understanding the dance between you

EFT sees relationship distress not as failure but as a protest against disconnection. Together we slow down the moments that go wrong, so that new, safer ways of reaching for each other can take hold.

Read about EFT
  1. 01

    Recognise the cycle

    We map the pattern that pulls you apart and learn to see it, rather than each other, as the problem.

  2. 02

    Reach what lies beneath

    We gently explore the fears and needs under the reactions, the parts that rarely get said.

  3. 03

    Build new moments of connection

    You practise turning toward each other in ways that feel safe and that last beyond the therapy room.
