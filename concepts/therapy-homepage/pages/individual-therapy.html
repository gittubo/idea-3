Is this for you?

You might be feeling…

You don't need to be in a relationship to do relationship-focused work. Many people come because of how they feel inside their connections, past or present.

  • Anxious in relationships, or afraid of being too much
  • Shut down or numb when things get close
  • Drawn into the same painful pattern with different partners
  • Grieving a relationship, a person or a version of your life
  • Unsettled by a big transition: a move, a breakup, becoming a parent
  • Carrying the weight of a difficult childhood or family
  • Lonely, even when you're surrounded by people

How it works

An approach built on attachment

I work with individuals using Emotionally Focused Individual Therapy (EFIT), an approach that grew out of EFT for couples. It's based on a simple idea: the ways we learned to seek comfort and protect ourselves early in life still shape how we feel and react today.

Together we notice the patterns that keep you stuck, gently make room for the feelings underneath them, and build a kinder, steadier relationship with yourself. Over time, that steadiness changes how you show up with others.

A typical session

Sessions are 50 minutes, in person in Surry Hills or online. Some people come weekly for a few months; others prefer fortnightly sessions over a longer period. We'll decide together and review it as we go.

Questions

Before you begin

Can I claim a Medicare rebate?

Psychotherapy with a PACFA-registered therapist isn't covered by Medicare. Some private health funds offer rebates for counselling; check with your fund about your level of cover.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on what you'd like to change. Some people come for eight to twelve sessions; others prefer longer, deeper work. We'll review progress together regularly.

Is what I share confidential?

Yes. Everything you share stays private, with a few legal exceptions, such as a serious risk to your safety or someone else's. I'll explain these in our first session.

What if I'm in crisis?

Kindred Room isn't a crisis service. If you need urgent help, call Lifeline on 13 11 14, or 000 in an emergency.
