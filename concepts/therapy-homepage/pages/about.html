My story

How I came to this work

I grew up in a big, loud, loving family in the Blue Mountains, where I learned early how much a single moment of being understood can change the shape of a day. Long before I had words for attachment, I was curious about why some conversations brought people closer and others pushed them apart.

I began my career in community mental health in Western Sydney, then spent seven years at a relationship counselling service in the inner west. That's where I first trained in Emotionally Focused Therapy, and where I watched couples who had nearly given up find their way back to each other.

“The question underneath almost every argument is the same: are you there for me?”

I opened Kindred Room in 2019 to create the kind of space I'd want to walk into myself: quiet, warm and unhurried, with time to understand what's really happening between people.

How I work

What you can expect from me

Warmth

No judgement, ever

Whatever brings you here, you'll be met with respect and genuine care. Nothing you share will shock me.

Structure

A clear way forward

EFT gives our work a map. You'll understand what we're doing in each session, and why.

Balance

Fair to both of you

In couples work I don't take sides. My job is to help each of you feel heard, especially when that's hard.

Pace

Gentle, not rushed

We go at a speed that feels safe. Slowing down is often where the real change happens.

Inclusion

All relationships welcome

I work with couples and individuals of all genders, sexualities, cultures and relationship structures.

Collaboration

You stay in charge

We agree on goals together and review them regularly. You can always tell me if something isn't working.
