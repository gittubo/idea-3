Sound familiar?

When love starts to feel like a battle

Most couples I see aren't short on love. They're caught in a pattern that has taken on a life of its own.

  • Small disagreements turn into the same big fight
  • One of you pursues and the other withdraws
  • You feel more like flatmates than partners
  • Trust has been broken by an affair or a betrayal
  • A new baby, illness or loss has pulled you apart
  • Intimacy has faded and it's hard to talk about
  • You're unsure whether to stay together, and want to decide with care

The process

What couples therapy looks like

Sessions one and two

Getting to know you both

We meet together to hear the story of your relationship, what's working and what hurts. I'll share my understanding of the pattern between you.

Individual sessions

One session each

Each partner has one individual session, so I can understand your history and anything that's hard to say in the room together.

The middle of the work

Slowing the cycle down

Using EFT, we de-escalate the conflict and help each of you share the softer feelings and needs underneath it.

Toward the end

Building new patterns

You practise reaching for each other in new ways, and we plan how to keep that connection strong after therapy ends.

After an affair

Rebuilding trust, one step at a time

Discovering an affair can feel like the ground has gone from under you. Both partners are often in shock, and it can be hard to know where to begin.

I use a structured approach to affair recovery that gives the hurt partner space to be heard and the partner who strayed a way to take responsibility and rebuild safety. Whether you decide to stay together or part with care, you won't have to work it out alone.

You don't have to know whether you want to stay together before you come.

Questions

What couples often ask

My partner isn't sure about therapy. What can I do?

That's very common. Your partner is welcome to join the free introductory call to ask questions first. Many hesitant partners feel more at ease once they know I won't take sides.

Do you work with LGBTQIA+ couples?

Yes. Kindred Room welcomes couples of all genders, sexualities and relationship structures.

Is couples therapy right if there's violence?

If there is violence or ongoing fear in the relationship, couples therapy isn't the safest first step. I can help you find specialist support. You can also call 1800RESPECT on 1800 737 732.

How long does couples therapy take?

Many couples come for twelve to twenty sessions, but it varies. We'll check in regularly about how it's going.
